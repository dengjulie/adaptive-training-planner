## What the app does

Train Smarter builds a training plan from your COROS workout history. Each morning it adjusts the plan based on your Oura readiness and on the sessions you completed or missed. Your sessions go on a dedicated "Training" calendar in your Google account. How your data is handled is described in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
